## Seven-day plan

| Day | Action | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Send the WhatsApp message to five people you actually know in 2BAC STE. Ask one question: "what topic is hardest?" | 15 min |
| 2 | Post the French message in **one** active Moroccan bac group. Reply to every comment. | 20 min |
| 3 | Email three maths or English teachers with the teacher template. | 20 min |
| 4 | Share one guide (limits or conditionals) in a class group with a one-line reason. | 10 min |
| 5 | Turn the most requested topic into five new questions and deploy. | 45 min |
| 6 | Post the Moroccan Arabic version in a different group; do not repost in the same one. | 15 min |
| 7 | Review replies: teacher interest, missing topics, confusion. Ask one teacher for the paid pilot. | 30 min |

## Content ideas that travel

- "Why does sin(x)/x tend to 1?" — one image, one sentence, link to the limits guide.
- "Three tenses students mix up in the 2BAC exam" — carousel, link to the tenses guide.
- "Can you solve this in 60 seconds?" — one exam question, answer in the comments, link in
  the bio.
- "Free printable worksheet for your class" — a photo of the printed sheet; teachers share
  this more than students do.

## What not to do

- Do not post the same message in ten groups on the same day. It reads as spam and gets
  you banned.
- Do not invent testimonials, user numbers or "trusted by" logos.
- Do not sell Premium before payments and server-side gating exist. The pricing page says
  it is not connected, and that stays true until Stripe is live.
- Do not promise a teacher dashboard that has not been built. The pilot is worksheets,
  exam simulation and topic requests.
- Do not collect student phone numbers or school data.

## What to measure

- Replies and questions per post, not likes.
- Teacher replies and pilot requests.
- Emails sent to the waitlist address.
- Which guide gets shared most (WhatsApp share button is on every guide).

There is no analytics script by design. If you want numbers later, add a cookie-free
analytics tool after checking its privacy terms, and update `/privacy/`.
